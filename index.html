About Us

The UbiSys Research Group, led by Dr. Suchetana Chakraborty, explores core areas of ubiquitous computing, activity recognition, health sensing, and low‑power embedded systems.

Lab Facilities: Sensepod++ testbed, Smartwatches, Earables, Smartphones, Embedded Boards (Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32), Wireless Modules (BLE, LoRa), Prototyping Kits, Edge‑AI Devices, MEMS & Biomedical Sensors, Environmental Sensors, High‑Performance Workstations, Audio Interfaces, mm‑Wave Tools, Cameras, Touch Displays, Mechanical Toolkits, Network Infrastructure.

Research Areas

Ubiquitous Computing

Systems that blend seamlessly into daily life environments.

Edge Computing & AIoT

Low‑latency intelligence at the network edge using sensors and embedded AI.

Human‑Centered Interfaces

Interfaces for wearable tech, accessibility, and human‑sensing.

Smart Health

Assistive and diagnostic technologies for personalized healthcare.

Autonomous Systems

Sensor‑fused platforms for mobility, drones, and intelligent monitoring.

Wireless & Sensor Networks

Communication, synchronization, and power‑efficiency in embedded systems.
